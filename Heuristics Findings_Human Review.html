HUMAN REVIEW · BASELINE

UX Audit Findings

A manual review of the dummy interface identified six deliberate usability and heuristic violations. These findings establish the human-reviewed baseline against which the AI audit methods can be evaluated.

Heuristics violated

6 findings · Human review
1

Heuristic #1: Visibility of System Status

The “Sync Database” button freezes the app for 15 seconds without a loading wheel or progress bar.

2

Heuristic #2: Match Between System and Real World

The error message (ERR_HEX_0x002F89) uses system-centered jargon instead of human-readable language.

3

Heuristic #3: User Control and Freedom

Clicking “Delete Account” instantly purges data without an “Are you sure?” confirmation step or an “Undo” safety net.

4

Heuristic #4: Consistency and Standards

The dangerous “Delete Account” button is styled in bright green, which traditionally signals “Go”, “Success”, or “Save”.

6

Heuristic #6: Recognition Rather Than Recall

The symbol button (⎋) in the top right has no text label or visible icon system standard, forcing users to guess its function.

10

Heuristic #10: Help and Documentation

The greyed-out micro-text at the top (#e1e1e1 on white) breaks accessibility contrast guidelines (WCAG), making it functionally invisible to many users.

Baseline status: These findings represent the human review used as the reference point for comparing AI-generated UX audits.
